## Who He Is

Simon Latham is a British songwriter, vocalist, and producer active in electronic music from 1990 to the present. His documented work spans UK progressive house, the progressive-to-trance transition of the late 1990s, independent label ownership, and later international releases as a featured vocalist.

The archive connects releases, broadcasts, chart records, interviews and clearly labelled first-person accounts that were previously distributed across separate sources.

## Documented Career Markers

- **Soundsation — “Do You Feel It?”** was identified by John Digweed as one of his favourite records of 1996 on BBC Radio 1, immediately after he discussed touring around America while promoting the *Northern Exposure* compilation.
- **Sasha** played the track at Cream in Liverpool.
- **Dave Seaman** selected it for *Renaissance: The Mix Collection Part 4* before the Stress Records deal was completed, according to Simon’s firsthand account.
- **Armada Music** included Simon on its *Best Vocalists of 2012* compilation.
- **Black Hole Recordings** included *Never Far* on *In Search of Sunrise 12*.
- **A State of Trance** broadcast vocal collaborations featuring Simon, including Episodes 424 and 802.
- **Airport Route Recordings** released early Kid Mistik material before the artist became internationally known as Bjarki.
- **Pole Folder** confirmed Simon’s lyrical role on the *Human EP* and stated that Simon’s friendship with GusGus led to their remix of *U.S.E.*

These markers establish participation across several periods and networks without treating every association as an equivalent collaboration or endorsement.

## Independent and First-Person Evidence

The archive separates externally checkable evidence from artist testimony:

- release credits, chart records and compilation track listings;
- surviving radio audio and documented DJ playlists;
- contemporary interviews and promotional reports;
- **Artist Story** sections for Simon’s firsthand memories;
- reported memories where an event has not been independently confirmed.

This distinction is important because much 1990s underground music activity was documented through physical records, broadcasts and specialist publications rather than searchable online sources.

## The Network in Context

Simon’s career intersects at different points with established progressive-house and trance figures and institutions:

- John Digweed and Sasha through documented support for *Do You Feel It?*
- Dave Seaman through Stress Records and the Renaissance compilation placement
- James Wiltshire through early studio work and the later Stress connection
- GusGus through Simon’s personal relationship with the group and the 2014 *U.S.E.* remix
- Armin van Buuren through SOS Recordings label context and later A State of Trance broadcasts
- Bjarki, credited as Kid Mistik, through Airport Route Recordings
- Pole Folder through the jointly credited *Human EP*

The nature and strength of each connection are described in the relevant article rather than inferred from name association alone.

## Documented Career Arc

Taken together, the sources show a continuous career: early songwriting and studio work, Soundsation’s progressive-house releases, the move toward trance, independent label activity in Iceland, and later vocal collaborations released and broadcast through established specialist platforms.

The purpose of this archive is to make that chronology discoverable while preserving the difference between documented fact, published opinion and personal recollection.
